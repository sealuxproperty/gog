Who we are This Privacy Policy is provided by Gameplay Over Graphics ("we," "us," or "our"), the developer of the mobile application available on Google Play. By downloading or using any of our apps you agree to the practices described in this policy. 2. Scope & audience (including children) Our app is designed for a mixed audience — it is accessible to users of all ages, including children under 13. Because we knowingly may have child users, we comply with the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play's Families Policy. We implement a neutral age screen at first launch. Users who indicate they are under 13 are routed into a restricted experience in which behavioural advertising, analytics profiling, and non-essential data collection are disabled. No personal information is collected from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent. 3. Data we collect 3a. Device identifiers & analytics data We collect pseudonymous device-level identifiers and usage statistics, including: App instance identifiers (Firebase Installation ID) Device type, operating system version, and language settings In-app events (screens viewed, features used, session length) Crash reports and performance diagnostics Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / GAID) — for adults only; disabled for users under 13 3b. Location data With your permission, we may collect approximate or precise location data to provide location-relevant features. Location is only collected while the app is in use (foreground). You can revoke this permission at any time via your device settings. Location data is never collected from users under 13. 3c. User-generated content (UGC) Content you create, submit, or share within the app (such as scores, usernames, profile customisations, or in-app messages) is stored on our servers to provide the service. UGC submitted by users who have identified as under 13 is subject to parental consent requirements before it is shared publicly. 3d. Purchase data In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not store or process your payment card information. We receive only a transaction confirmation and the items purchased, which are used to fulfil your order. 4. How we use your data To operate, maintain, and improve the app and its features To analyse usage patterns and fix crashes (via Firebase Analytics) To serve relevant advertisements to adult users (via Google AdMob) To fulfil in-app purchases and restore them across devices To provide location-based features where you have granted permission To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service We do not sell your personal data to third parties. 5. Third-party services Our app integrates the following third-party services. Each operates under its own privacy policy: Google Analytics for Firebase — usage analytics and crash reporting. Google Privacy Policy Google AdMob — advertising (adult users only). Google Privacy Policy Google Play Billing — in-app purchase processing. Google Privacy Policy These services may collect data independently subject to their own terms. We configure them to respect the age status of each user. 6. Advertising & tracking Personalised advertising powered by Google AdMob is shown only to users aged 13 and over who have not opted out. For users identified as under 13, AdMob is configured in child-directed mode, which disables interest-based and behavioural advertising and prevents the collection of persistent identifiers for profiling purposes. You may opt out of personalised ads at any time by visiting Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device and enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalisation." 7. User-generated content Content you create within the app may be visible to other users depending on the feature (e.g., leaderboards or community features). Do not include personal information in your username, messages, or other public-facing content. We reserve the right to remove content that violates our community standards. You may request deletion of your UGC by contacting us (see Section 15). 8. Location data Location data is used solely to provide in-app features that require it (such as regional leaderboards or location-based challenges). It is not sold, shared for advertising purposes, or retained beyond the session unless required for a specific feature you have enabled. You can disable location access at any time in your device settings without loss of core app functionality. 9. Data retention We retain your data only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected: Analytics data — retained for up to 14 months, then automatically deleted by Firebase. User-generated content — retained while your account is active; deleted within 30 days of an account deletion request. Location data — not retained beyond the active session unless tied to a saved feature. Children's data — we retain only the minimum data necessary and delete it promptly once the purpose has been fulfilled or upon parental request. 10. Data security We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit (TLS) and access controls on our servers. While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for any account features and to keep your device's operating system up to date. 11. Your rights & choices Depending on your location, you may have the right to: Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you. Correction — request that inaccurate data be corrected. Deletion — request that your data be deleted ("right to be forgotten"). Restriction — ask us to limit how we process your data. Opt-out of sale/sharing — we do not sell personal data; however, California residents may exercise CCPA rights by contacting us. Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (EU/UK users). To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in Section 15. We will respond within 30 days. 12. Children's privacy (COPPA) We comply with COPPA and Google Play's Families Policy. Specifically: We use a neutral age screen to identify users under 13 before any data is collected. We do not collect personal information from users under 13 without verifiable parental consent. We do not show behavioural or interest-based advertising to users under 13. We do not share personal information of users under 13 with third parties for marketing purposes. Parents may review, correct, or request deletion of their child's data at any time by contacting us (see Section 15). We will respond within 10 business days. We retain children's personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13 without parental consent, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly. 13. International data transfers Our servers and third-party service providers may be located outside your country of residence, including in the United States. By using the app, you consent to the transfer of your data to these countries, which may have different data protection laws than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (such as Standard Contractual Clauses for EU data transfers) in accordance with applicable law. 14. Changes to this policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top and, where required, notify you via an in-app notice or by other means before the changes take effect. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. 15.
