FortiFi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10/11/2026

1. Who We Are

FortiFi is developed and published by Gameplay Over Graphics ("we", "us" or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you play FortiFi.

For privacy-related questions, contact us at gameplayographics@gmail.com.

2. Who Can Play FortiFi

FortiFi is designed for a general audience and may be played by people of different ages, including children.

We do not currently require players to create a separate account, provide their name or email address, or complete an age-screening process within the game.

3. Information Collected Through Third-Party Services

FortiFi uses services provided by Google. These services may collect or process information automatically when you play the game.

Google AdMob: FortiFi displays advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. Google may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interactions, device information and diagnostic information to provide and measure advertising. The precise information collected depends on Google's services, your device and the applicable settings.

Google Play Games Services: FortiFi uses Google Play Games Services for its global leaderboards. Scores and associated game information may be processed by Google to operate this feature. Leaderboard results are currently displayed anonymously within FortiFi: other players see rankings and scores, not player names.

These services are provided by Google and may process information in accordance with Google's own privacy policies and settings. We do not control all information Google collects or how Google independently uses it.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob information.

4. Advertising

FortiFi uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Advertisements are currently optional. Players may choose to watch rewarded advertisements in exchange for in-game rewards. FortiFi does not currently require players to watch advertisements to access worlds, stages or other game content.

Google may use information to select, deliver and measure advertisements, subject to its policies and applicable settings. We do not personally select individual advertisements shown in the game.

If our advertising arrangements change in a future version, we will update this Policy as appropriate and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law or platform rules.

5. Children's Privacy

FortiFi is designed for a general audience, including children. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information directly through the game.

However, third-party services such as Google AdMob and Google Play Games Services may process information when the game is used. Their collection and use of information is governed by their own policies and applicable settings.

Parents or guardians who have concerns about information associated with a child's use of FortiFi can contact us at gameplayographics@gmail.com.

6. Information We Do Not Currently Request

FortiFi does not currently include:

  • A separate player registration or login system.
  • In-game chat, private messaging or user-posted content.
  • Location-based features or a requirement to provide location information.
  • In-app purchases.

This describes the game's current functionality and may change in future versions.

7. Data Retention and Security

Information processed through Google services is subject to Google's retention and security practices. We do not control the retention periods or security procedures applied independently by Google.

We take reasonable steps within our control to protect the game and any information we handle. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to request access, correction or deletion, or to object to certain processing.

Where information is processed by Google through its services, you may need to exercise your rights directly through Google. You can review Google's privacy information and account settings at Google Account.

You can also contact us at gameplayographics@gmail.com with questions or requests relating to FortiFi.

9. International Data Transfers

Google may process information on servers located in countries outside your own. Any such processing is subject to Google's policies and applicable data protection requirements.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its features or its use of third-party services changes. The latest version will be published on this page, with the updated date shown at the top.
